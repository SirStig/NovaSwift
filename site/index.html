The game

The galaxy you remember. Played the way it was.

Trade, fight, take sides in a war and follow six storylines across hundreds of systems. NOVA Swift reads your own copy of EV Nova and runs it on a new engine, so the ships, missions and story are the real ones.

Checked against the original

The Windows executable was decompiled and every gameplay system compared against it: flight, combat, AI, economy, missions, the map. Original behaviour is the default; anything new is an opt-in Enhancement.

Multiplayer Beta

Your galaxy. Their galaxy. Same star system.

Multiplayer is an overlay on your own game. Everyone keeps their own save and roams freely; when two pilots meet in a system, that system syncs completely. "I'm stuck, come help me" is one jump away.

On Apple devices, play over Game Center or local Wi-Fi and find open lobbies in the public list. On Linux, Steam Deck and Windows, join by address, browse LAN games, or pick a community server. Cross-play between the two is being wired up now.

Plug-ins

Twenty years of mods, one tap away.

Browse the community catalog in the game and install with one tap, or import your own files: old Mac .sit and .hqx archives included. Total conversions, new ships, story add-ons and HD art. Turn any of them off again with a switch.

Plug-in editor

An editor that runs the game.

Edit every resource type, draw the galaxy, lay out systems over a live preview, then press Test Run and fly your plug-in with a debug panel: spawn fleets, grant outfits, set control bits, start missions.

And more

Small things that add up.

  • Story GuideEvery campaign as steps: what's done, what's locked and which mission opens the next. Reads plug-in storylines too.
  • Your old pilotsImport Windows pilots straight into the game, classic Mac pilots in testing. iCloud keeps Apple devices in step.
  • Classic or modernThe original interface, or the Enhanced and Nova Swift looks. Then switch the map, menus or HUD individually.
  • HD art and 3DPlug-ins can bring high-resolution art and live 3D ships that bank and turn. Hit boxes stay the same.
  • Debug suiteSearchable live logs, frame time per subsystem, an inspector and a console. Bug reports attach your logs.
  • Controller action wheelsHold a shoulder button for weapons, targets and commands. Every menu is reachable with a pad.
Fidelity

671 of 671 functions compared.

Every portable function of the original game has been compared with NOVA Swift and fixed to match, or replaced on purpose where it has to be (the save format, the controls). That's how much has been checked, not a promise there are no bugs; NOVA Swift is in beta.

If something plays differently from the original, it's a bug. Report it.
